OpenTS2 - an open engine for Toy Story 2
========================================

Please read before installing.

UNOFFICIAL FAN PROJECT

OpenTS2 is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or
connected to Disney, Pixar, Traveller's Tales or Activision. Toy Story 2 and its
characters are their property.

YOU NEED THE ORIGINAL GAME

OpenTS2 contains no game data: no levels, models, textures, sounds, music, movies
or code from the original game (its icon, a picture of the game's Pizza Planet
token, is the one exception). It plays from your own installed copy of the PC
game "Toy Story 2: Buzz Lightyear to the Rescue" (1999), which it reads but never
changes. For music and movies it can also use the game disc.

WHAT IS INSTALLED

- OpenTS2 (the launcher) and ts2-viewer (the engine), with Start menu and optional
  desktop shortcuts.
- Your settings and mods are kept in %APPDATA%\OpenTS2, and saves in
  %APPDATA%\ts2-rust. Uninstalling OpenTS2 leaves both in place.
